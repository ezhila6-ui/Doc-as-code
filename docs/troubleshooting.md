# Troubleshooting

Use this section to resolve common SecureAware issues.

## Users do not receive campaign emails

Verify the following:

- The campaign is active.
- The user has a valid email address.
- The SecureAware sending domains are allowlisted.
- The message was not delivered to the user's spam folder.

If the issue continues, contact your SecureAware administrator.

## Campaign remains in Scheduled status

Confirm that the scheduled date and time have passed.

Also verify that the campaign contains at least one user.
